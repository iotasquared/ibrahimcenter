# Visit

## Prayer at Ibrahim Center

We gather for Jumu'ah — the Friday congregational prayer — every Friday at 1:30 PM, and the coffee shop is open before and after. We do not currently hold daily congregational prayers at the center.

## Location & parking

Ibrahim Center · 618 Baca Street, Houston, Texas 77013. Parking is available on-site and along surrounding neighborhood streets. Please be mindful of our neighbors and avoid blocking driveways or restricted areas.

Come in through the double doors — a volunteer will be nearby to point you the rest of the way.

## Restrooms & accessibility

The brothers' restroom is located near the courtyard. The sisters' restroom is located behind the privacy curtain in the hallway nearest the musalla (prayer space). If you need assistance locating either restroom, please ask a volunteer or member of our team.

## Families

Families and children are warmly welcomed at Ibrahim Center. We strive to cultivate an environment where young people develop a love for the masjid while parents and families find community, support, and belonging.
