# Biography

Hamida Mitha earned her Bachelor's degree in Liberal Arts and Islamic Studies with a minor in Arabic from Zaytuna College. She has a decade of experience serving in marketing, programs, events, and community care for Muslim-led non-profits across the United States including ALIM, IMAN, Ta'leef, Zaytuna College, Maristan, and Amaanah Refugee Services.

Hamida is passionate about true community building and healing on an individual and societal level. In her free time, she can be found exploring Houston parks, reflecting on Allah, and researching the spiritual and theological frameworks of human relationships, healing, and the natural sciences. She aspires to merge her passion for research and community service to help deepen the Muslim community's collective God consciousness.
