# Young Stewards Sunday School

Young Stewards is our Sunday school, part of the [Little Stewards](/programs/little-stewards/) family of children's programs. Children gather on Sundays to learn their faith in a warm, steady environment, alongside friends and teachers who know them by name.

We are finalizing the details for the coming year — ages, schedule, and how to register. If you have a child you would like to enroll, [get connected](/get-connected/) and we will reach out with everything as soon as registration opens.
