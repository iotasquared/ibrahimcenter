Shaykh Khalis Rashaad

Imam & Resident Scholar

Shaykh Khalis Rashaad serves as the Imam and Resident Scholar of Ibrahim Center, where he provides spiritual guidance, teaches sacred knowledge, and helps cultivate a community rooted in faith, companionship, and service. Through his leadership, Ibrahim Center has become a space where traditional Islamic learning, spiritual growth, and meaningful relationships come together.

His areas of study include Islamic theology (aqidah), jurisprudence (fiqh), spirituality (tasawwuf), Qur'anic exegesis (tafsir), and applied theology. He holds ijazat in jurisprudence, hadith, theology, and spirituality, and continues advanced doctoral research in Islamic jurisprudence with a focus on the practical application of Islamic law.

As the founder and lead instructor of the Dawam Seminary for Islamic Studies, Shaykh Khalis teaches students to engage the Islamic scholarly tradition with both depth and clarity. His teaching emphasizes applied Islamic knowledge, helping Muslims navigate contemporary questions and modern life through the framework of Islamic law, theology, and spirituality.

A significant focus of his work centers on the realities facing Muslims living in the West. Through his classes, lectures, and community programs, he addresses questions surrounding identity, family, purpose, faith, ethics, and spiritual well-being.

Shaykh Khalis also leads Menaissance, Ibrahim Center's men's initiative dedicated to reviving healthy masculinity, spiritual responsibility, and sacred brotherhood. Beyond the masjid, he has spent years engaged in community outreach, mentorship, and educational initiatives.

In addition to his scholarly pursuits, Shaykh Khalis has a longstanding background in martial arts, boxing, and kickboxing. Those who know him often speak of his warmth, hospitality, and genuine interest in people. He believes sacred knowledge is best transmitted through sincere human relationships.

Shaykh Khalis also runs Ihsan Hub, an online digital learning space dedicated to bringing traditional Islamic learning into modern life.

Amna Mulla

Executive Director

Amna is the Executive Director of Ibrahim Center. She is a native Houstonian and a mother of two children. She graduated from the University of Houston with a degree in supply chain management and information systems. She began her career in San Francisco in consulting, working across various industries before transitioning into the tech sector, where she organized and structured various start-ups during periods of massive growth. While in the Bay Area, Amna served the Muslim community at Taleef, where she learned about convert care and holding space for an authentic community.

In 2018, Amna and her family moved back to Texas, where she founded Ruh Karima, a women's art collective. As the art director, Amna led various installations. Although Ruh Karima is currently on pause, Amna is excited to bring her experiential design expertise to IIC.

Amna has been a key member of our community for five years. During this time, she developed the Little Stewards children's program (previously known as Suhba Camp) and played a crucial role in organizing and executing our largest Ramadan fundraiser.

Amna's vision for IIC is to maintain our community's intimate and supportive environment while fostering growth and visibility. She aims to create a space where everyone, including families, elders, and youth, feels at home.

amna@ibrahimcenter.org

A.J. Qureshi

Chief of Staff / Associate Director

A.J. is a native Houstonian with a deep passion for people and community. Known for his warm and inviting personality and love for building meaningful connections, he strives to make Ibrahim Center a welcoming home for everyone who walks through its doors.

With a background in counseling, psychology, and human development, he finds purpose in being of service to others through guidance, encouragement, and simply being present. Whether hosting events as an emcee, helping organize programs, or walking alongside others in their personal growth, A.J approaches his work with a heart for service.

He is currently pursuing a Master's degree in Clinical Mental Health Counseling & Psychology, reflecting his ongoing commitment to supporting the emotional, spiritual, and personal growth of others.

His goal is to help create an environment where faith and friendship grow side by side, and where every member of the community feels seen, supported, and truly at home.

aj@ibrahimcenter.org

Fatima Lakhani

Youth Director; Lead Educator & Program Coordinator, Little Stewards

Fatima Lakhani serves as the Youth Director, lead educator, and program coordinator for Little Stewards at Ibrahim Center, helping cultivate spaces where children and families can develop a lasting love for Islam, community, and the masjid.

Her work focuses on creating engaging, developmentally appropriate learning experiences that introduce children to faith through storytelling, creativity, play, and meaningful relationships. Through puppetry, crafts, interactive activities, and hands-on learning, she helps young Muslims build connections to Allah, the Prophets, the Companions, and the beauty of God's creation.

Fatima has played an integral role in the growth and development of Little Stewards, helping establish a welcoming environment where children feel safe, valued, and excited to learn. Her work emphasizes curiosity, compassion, confidence, and a sense of belonging within the Muslim community.

She has also helped lead children's programming during family retreats and special events, creating experiences that encourage teamwork, exploration, and spiritual growth while helping children engage with the world through love, wonder, and faith.

Through her dedication to children and families, Fatima continues to help Ibrahim Center nurture the next generation of Muslims by creating spaces where learning, joy, and spiritual growth go hand in hand.

fatima@ibrahimcenter.org

Meleekah Villegas

Community Care Coordinator

Meleekah is the Community Care Coordinator of Ibrahim Center. She is a Tejana born and raised in the west part of Houston, TX, and moved to 3rd Ward in 2001. She is an activist, community researcher, and artist working on issues related to health, wellness, and community development in Houston's Third Ward community. Nurtured by service, mentors, and motivators, she develops her passion for transformation and renewal through the use of creative methods such as photography, drama therapy, and outdoor activation. She enjoys creating space for communal conversations, exploration, and healing as a creative connector to support socioeconomically disadvantaged communities through the Arts, Culture, and Care.

meleekah@ibrahimcenter.org

Hamida Mitha

Community Fellow

Hamida Mitha earned her Bachelor's degree in Liberal Arts and Islamic Studies with a minor in Arabic from Zaytuna College. She has a decade of experience serving in marketing, programs, events, and community care for Muslim-led non-profits across the United States including ALIM, IMAN, Ta'leef, Zaytuna College, Maristan, and Amaanah Refugee Services.

Hamida is passionate about true community building and healing on an individual and societal level. In her free time, she can be found exploring Houston parks, reflecting on Allah, and researching the spiritual and theological frameworks of human relationships, healing, and the natural sciences. She aspires to merge her passion for research and community service to help deepen the Muslim community's collective God consciousness.

hamida@ibrahimcenter.org
